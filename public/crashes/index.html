Crash Reporting

Standing General Order Crash Reports

Since 2021, Standing General Order 2021-01 has required the makers and operators of vehicles equipped with automated driving systems (ADS) or Level 2 advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) to report certain crashes to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

This is every report in NHTSA’s published files, current and archived, each at its latest version, narratives and redactions as filed. Search it. Filter it.

Data National Highway Traffic Safety Administration

NHTSA’s notes on the data

From the agency’s data dictionary for these files:

  • “Reporting requirements are different for Automated Driving Systems (ADS) and Level 2 Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS).”
  • “Access to crash data may affect crash reporting.”
  • “Incident report data may be incomplete or unverified.”
  • “Indicated Confidential Business Information (CBI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) are redacted.”
  • “The same crash may have multiple reports.”
  • “Summary incident report data are not normalized.”

System is the file NHTSA published a report in: ADS, Level 2 ADAS, or Other. Period is the set of files: the 2021–2025 archive, or the current files begun under the order’s third amendment. Filings stating that an entity had no new or updated reports are not included.
