Stock Trades

Congressional Stock Trades

The STOCK Act (2012) requires members of Congress to publicly disclose stock trades within 45 days. The Senate publishes these as structured electronic filings. The House publishes PDFs.

71,130 transactions from 382 members of Congress, 2012–2026, from filings through Nov 17, 2026. From the House Clerk’s and the Senate’s electronic filings since 2014.

Data U.S. House Clerk periodic transaction reports; U.S. Senate electronic filings (eFD)

Paper filings

3,066 filings were submitted on paper as scanned images and are not transcribed. Each links to its scan.

    Restrictions on use

    The transactions come from financial disclosure reports filed with the Clerk of the House and with the Senate, the Senate’s from eFD, its electronic disclosure site. A filing eFD no longer serves is recorded from Senate Stock Watcher. Federal law limits how the reports may be used, 5 U.S.C. § 13107(c)(1):

    “It shall be unlawful for any person to obtain or use a report— (A) for any unlawful purpose; (B) for any commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media for dissemination to the general public; (C) for determining or establishing the credit rating of any individual; or (D) for use, directly or indirectly, in the solicitation of money for any political, charitable, or other purpose.”
