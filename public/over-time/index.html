How words are counted
  • A word counts in all its forms: “liberty” and “liberties” count together, as a search of the corpus finds them.
  • A year’s words are every word in its records, a hyphenated word counted once. Each part of a hyphenated word also counts as a use, so “self-government” is a use of “government”.
  • Very common words such as “the” are not counted. Each box takes one word.
  • Which records belong to a series is set by the list named under the figure.
