Statement of Disbursements

Each quarter the House’s Chief Administrative Officer publishes the disbursements of every member office, committee and House office: payee, description, date and amount, line by line.

This is every quarter of those detail files since January 2016, as the House filed them, with staff pay at the subtotals the House reports for each office.

Twice a year the Secretary of the Senate publishes the expenditures of every senator’s office, committee and Senate office: payee, description, dates and amount, line by line.

This is every volume of that report since April 2011, as the Senate printed it, with staff pay at the subtotals the Senate prints for each office.

Data U.S. House of Representatives, Chief Administrative Officer Secretary of the Senate

How the House files them
  • Staff pay and benefits to former personnel are shown as the House’s own subtotal for each office, program and quarter, not line by line.
  • Payees, descriptions and office names are as filed. One vendor can appear under several spellings.
  • The office is the filed organization name without its year. The full name is in each row’s record.
  • Amounts are as filed. Credits and refunds are negative.
  • A date the House filed that is not a calendar date is shown as filed.
  • The House publishes July 2009 to December 2015 as PDF volumes. Those quarters are not here yet.
How the Senate prints them
  • Staff pay is shown as the Senate’s own subtotal for each office and class of pay, not person by person.
  • Payees, descriptions and office names are as printed. One vendor can appear under several spellings.
  • The office is the name at the head of each statement, as printed. An office with funds from more than one year has a statement for each.
  • Amounts are as printed. Credits and refunds are negative.
  • Where a statement’s summary charges an amount to a class of spending that its lines do not list, that amount is a row of its own.
  • A few statements’ lines do not add up to the summary printed above them. The lines are here as printed.
  • A date printed in a form that is not a calendar date is shown as printed.
