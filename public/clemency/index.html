Executive Clemency

Clemency Recipients

The Justice Department’s Office of the Pardon Attorney lists the people each president has granted clemency, by pardon or by commutation of sentence, from Richard Nixon to the present.

This is every entry on those lists, names, districts, offenses and sentences as DOJ listed them, with the recipients DOJ names for two group commutations.

Data U.S. Department of Justice, Office of the Pardon Attorney

How DOJ lists them
  • The lists change form across administrations. Through Ronald Reagan they give the date, name and district; later lists add the offense and sentence, and most since 2017 link each warrant.
  • A value in several parts keeps DOJ’s numbering, one part to a line.
  • Biden’s commutations of December 12, 2024 and January 17, 2025 are one line each on DOJ’s list. Their recipients are from DOJ’s detail page for each grant.
  • Two grants in 2025 DOJ lists by title alone, with no names. Each is one entry here, typed “Listed by title,” and links its Federal Register proclamation where the titles match.
  • A footnote DOJ marks with an asterisk is carried on the entries it marks.
