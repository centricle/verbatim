U.S. House of Representatives

Statement of Disbursements

Each quarter the House’s Chief Administrative Officer publishes the disbursements of every member office, committee and House office: payee, description, date and amount, line by line.

This is every quarter of those detail files since January 2016, as the House filed them, with staff pay at the subtotals the House prints for each office.

Data U.S. House of Representatives, Chief Administrative Officer

How the House files them
  • Staff pay and benefits to former personnel are shown as the House’s own subtotal for each office, program and quarter, not line by line.
  • Payees, descriptions and office names are as filed. One vendor can appear under several spellings.
  • The office is the filed organization name without its year. The full name is in each row’s record.
  • Amounts are as filed. Credits and refunds are negative.
  • A date the House filed that is not a calendar date is shown as filed.
  • The House publishes July 2009 to December 2015 as PDF volumes. Those quarters are not here yet.
