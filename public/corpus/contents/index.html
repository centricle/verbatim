Contents of the Corpus

Every speaker in the corpus, listed under each official roster that names them and in the roster’s own words, with the number of records and the years they span. Then the State of the Union and inaugural addresses, member by member, as their cited lists give them.

Rosters The White House, the Supreme Court, the Biographical Directory of the United States Congress, the U.S. Senate

How speakers are listed
  • A speaker appears under every roster that lists them, so a president who served in Congress appears twice. Names are in surname order.
  • The words under each name are the roster’s: the Biographical Directory’s description, the heading the Supreme Court lists a justice under, or the name of the Senate’s list for a state.
  • The Biographical Directory is read from the copy kept by the House’s History, Art & Archives, which lists Representatives and Continental Congress delegates. Senate service comes from the Senate’s own state lists.
  • Counts include undated records. Years are the first and last dated record.
