Presidential Documents

Executive Orders and Proclamations

The executive orders and proclamations published in the Federal Register’s Presidential Documents section.

Each document is the text as the Federal Register published it, with its number, the dates it was signed and published, and its citation. Where the Federal Register notes that a later document revoked, amended or superseded it, the note is shown as written, and each order it names that is here links to its record. The same presidents’ speeches are in the Corpus. Search it. Filter it.

Text Office of the Federal Register

How these documents are listed
  • Numbers, dates, citations, titles and notes are the Federal Register’s, as published, typos included. A document the Federal Register gives no signing date has none here: it is listed and searchable, and not plotted by year.
  • An order or proclamation a note names links to its record when the record is here. Documents signed before 1994 are not.
  • A document the Federal Register published twice is listed once.
  • From the Federal Register: “It is not an official legal edition of the Federal Register, and does not replace the official print version or the official electronic version on GPO’s govinfo.gov.” Each record links to its printed page.
