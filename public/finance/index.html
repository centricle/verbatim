Every federal candidate who filed with the FEC for the 2024 cycle. President, Senate, House. How much they raised, how much they spent, where the money came from. The financial infrastructure of American democracy, in one table.

3,800+ candidates. Billions of dollars. Individual contributions, PAC money, party support, self-funding. Search by name, filter by party, office, or state.

Data Federal Election Commission

About this dataset

Source: Federal Election Commission bulk data (weball24.txt). Covers all candidates who filed financial reports for the 2023–2024 election cycle through December 31, 2024.

Incumbent status: I = Incumbent, C = Challenger, O = Open seat. Party codes: DEM = Democrat, REP = Republican, LIB = Libertarian, GRE = Green, etc.
